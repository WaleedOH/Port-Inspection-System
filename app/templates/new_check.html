1. Photograph the paperwork

Most VIN stickers (door frame, windscreen) have a barcode. Scanning it is far more reliable than reading the printed characters.

Tips: lay the page flat, fill the frame, avoid glare. Reader: {{ reader.name }}.

2. Check the details

How the factory built this car (from the VIN)

Badges, wheels and trim parts can be swapped; the VIN cannot. Compare these details with the actual vehicle: engine size and cylinders, drive type, body, and interior grade. Check that the VIN is the same on the dashboard, the door frame and the chassis stamp.
